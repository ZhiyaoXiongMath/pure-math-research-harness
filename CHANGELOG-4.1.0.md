# PMRS 4.1.0

- Added native SURVEY S0–S7 for scoped literature investigation, reading, shared knowledge organization, independent readiness checks, frozen writing plans and reviewed delivery.
- Separated Knowledge only from optional English manuscripts. Survey requests one selected minimal, thematic or lecture profile; TRW/TFDW keep their paired-report rules.
- Added controlled host retrieval with versioned sources and bounded failure handling, without enabling unrestricted network access in research tasks.
- Added English-default and Simplified Chinese interface resources with host-persisted preferences; research bodies and formal reports remain English.
- Extended diagrams, activity display and recovery-aware controls to three workflows while retaining existing research and model policies.
- Made the complete English README the default and added the complete Chinese README, six workflow diagrams and three pasteable teaching examples.
- Updated product and installation metadata to 4.1.0 while keeping native data-format identifiers and 4.x data locations stable.

See [validation](TEST-REPORT-4.1.0.md), [scope](ACCEPTANCE-SCOPE-4.1.0.md) and [installation](UBUNTU-E2E-GUIDE.md). BUILD-INFO.json, FINAL-ARTIFACT-AUDIT.json and SHA256SUMS.txt bind the final source and Release artifacts. Previous v4.0.0 artifacts remain available separately.
